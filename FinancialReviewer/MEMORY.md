## ROOTS framework
**R**evenue · **O**verhead · **O**perations · **T**ax · **S**urplus
- **Revenue:** Did they hit the revenue goal? Invoices sent vs. cash actually collected.
- **Overhead:** Did fixed overhead stay on budget, and within the metric relative to cash collected?
- **Operations:** Did variable ops stay within their % metric, or run past cash collected?
- **Tax:** Tax set-aside estimate based on net profit on cash — corporate level, plus individual level if pass-through.
- **Surplus:** What's actually left after everything. Break down: invoices sent → cash collected → recurring debt → recurring overhead → average ops → money leaks (spending that doesn't need to happen) → take-home after tax.

## Tiers
### Monthly tier
- Who: construction, home organizers (and other MRR clients on monthly plans).
- Pain: blind spots, cash leaks, what to focus on next month.
- Report: **one-page ROOTS summary**, monthly. Tracks invoices sent AND cash collected.

### Quarterly tier (also called "basic")
- Who: freelancers, entertainment; generally under $500K/yr.
- Pain: staying compliant, allocating resources correctly, lowest legal tax bill.
- Cash basis only — track cash received, NOT invoices.
- Report: ROOTS as a **scorecard** — cash received vs. % metrics/budget. Are they spending more than metrics allow?
- Lens: resource allocation across overhead, ops, tax, surplus to create more revenue growth.
- Standing goal: **grow cash collection 1% per month.** Recommend best approach for that, and/or where to put surplus to move into the growth stage.

### Business Management tier
- Who: $1M+/yr, OR profitability tied to projects/jobs.
- Monthly report: ROOTS at **company level + job profitability level.** Which clients/customers/jobs make the most money and most profit. What worked and what didn't this month → what to improve next month. Check cash spent per job and per company stays within cash collected.
- **Quarterly 90-day review:** identify the #1 constraint from 5 categories — **Leads, Conversions, Capacity, Cash, Money Leakage.** That constraint sets the next quarter's focus; monthly reports measure progress against it.

## Report standards (every tier)
- Start with compliance and bookkeeping check.
- 3–5 action items max — aim for 3. Each is one clear sentence leading to a solution.
- 5th-grade language. No accounting jargon.
- Say what's hurting them AND how it affects the business going forward if not changed.
- Flow: big picture → clear, outcome-driven decisions.
- One action/directive per line. One subject per section. Never combine people or topics in one sentence/paragraph.
- Client summary = **one-page PDF.**
- Hates: over-explaining, big paragraphs, info unrelated to the task.

## Compliance corrections format
- Bullet list, copy-paste ready for the bookkeeping team.
- Name the exact issue per line, e.g. missing vendor name, missing customer name, miscategorized transaction, uncleared transaction, balance sheet account off.
- Anything unresolved that affects cash → tag **REVIEW** and keep raising it every review until fixed (e.g. uncleared transactions, incorrect balance sheet).
